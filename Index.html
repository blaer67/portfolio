A booking, an enquiry, a sign-up or simply trust. I decide the job before I write the first line.

03

Facts before flourish

Good copy is accurate first and clever second. Every claim is checked, and every source is credible.

Questions you might have

What do you need to get started?

The topic or page, your target audience, any keywords you already have, a competitor example and your deadline. If you don’t have keywords yet, I’ll research them.

What kinds of content do you write?

Blogs and articles, website and landing page copy, Wikipedia articles, video scripts and social media posts, for both B2B and B2C audiences.

Do you do keyword research?

Yes. I research keywords and search intent before I write, then optimize titles, headings and links so the page can rank.

Can you publish to WordPress?

Yes. I manage WordPress and can upload finished posts with titles, headings and links set up.

Do you write Wikipedia articles?

Yes, for businesses and public figures. I write in a neutral tone with credible sources. Wikipedia’s editors decide what stays, so I write to its notability and sourcing rules.

Can you build backlinks?

Yes. I’ve built quality backlinks through outreach, keyword research and link-building strategies for a real estate company.

Which languages do you write in?

I write in English, and I’ve also written Hinglish scripts for Instagram and YouTube.

Do you take long-term work as well as one-off projects?

Both. I’ve delivered one-off pages and projects, and long-term work such as full-time blog writing.

Remote is my first choice and I work with clients worldwide. I’m open to on-site roles when the role is the right fit.
